## 📌 Featured Projects

### 1. [VibeAtlas](https://github.com/riddhicodesavvy/VibeAtlas)
*Acoustic Feature Exploration & Unsupervised Clustering Platform*
- **Stack**: Python, Scikit-Learn, SciPy, FastAPI, TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS.
- **Methodology**: Evaluates unsupervised clustering on the Free Music Archive (FMA) dataset across K-Means ($k=2\dots 10$), Gaussian Mixture Models (diagonal covariance, AIC/BIC, EM convergence), PCA dimensionality reduction, and Factor Analysis.
- **Reproducibility**: Enforces artist-disjoint train/validation/test partitions, deterministic random seeding, and immutable numeric artifact serialization. Verified by a 46-test automated suite (8 ML, 33 backend, 5 integration).

### 2. [BhaashaBandhu](https://github.com/riddhicodesavvy/bhaashaBandhu)
*Multilingual Workforce Communication & Safety Prototype (UN SDG 8)*
- **Stack**: React, TypeScript, Express.js, Supabase PostgreSQL with RLS, Firebase Auth, Groq LLMs (Llama 3.3 & Whisper STT).
- **Architecture**: Applied research prototype providing Indic translation across English, Hindi, and Bengali with prompt-boundary isolation, speech-to-text incident reporting, BOCW Act Section 28 overtime math modeling, and structured clinical SBAR triage handovers.
- **Verification**: Evaluated with 23 unit/integration tests and backend role-based access control (RBAC).

### 3. [Enterprise RAG System](https://github.com/riddhicodesavvy/RAG)
*Retrieval-Augmented Generation Architecture for Technical Documentation*
- **Stack**: Python, LangChain, ChromaDB, Sentence-Transformers (`all-MiniLM-L6-v2`), PyPDF / PyMuPDF.
- **Architecture**: End-to-end document indexing, recursive character chunking, dense vector retrieval, and context-grounded question answering. Serves as a public architectural demonstrator of methodologies developed during vocational training at Bridge & Roof Co. (India) Ltd.

### 4. [JA Assure — Research Agent Backend](https://github.com/riddhicodesavvy/Ja_assure_vertex)
*Multi-Stage Evidence Processing & Research Intelligence Pipeline*
- **Stack**: Python, FastAPI, Groq LLM, Tavily Search API, Supabase PostgreSQL.
- **Architecture**: Orchestrates multi-stage research workflows (search, extraction, normalization, deduplication, change detection) to synthesize structured findings with provenance-tracked citations and anti-hallucination verification.

### 5. [JalSathi](https://github.com/riddhicodesavvy/JalSathi)
*Water Quality Analysis & Machine Learning Classification*
- **Stack**: Python, Scikit-Learn, FastAPI, React, Vite.
- **Methodology**: Implements and benchmarks five genuine classification architectures — Decision Tree (CART), Random Forest, AdaBoost, Gradient Boosting, and a Soft-Voting Ensemble — on water potability data, evaluating confusion matrices, ROC curves, and cross-validation metrics.

### 6. [BankRakshak](https://github.com/riddhicodesavvy/BankRakshak)
*Banking Resilience & Isolated Disaster Recovery Platform*
